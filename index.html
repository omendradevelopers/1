Welcome to ODPL

ODPL is a design and construction company that brings interior design and its execution together under one roof. We plan every space with care and deliver it with precision, so that what you imagine is exactly what gets built.

Our team of designers, engineers and site professionals works closely with every client, from the first sketch to the final handover. Be it an office, a hospital or a hospitality space, our promise stays the same: We Build Better.

Why ODPL

We bring design and construction together. Our team plans every detail with care and delivers it with precision, so your space is built better.

Vision & Mission

We believe in honest work and the best quality for every client. We aim to build spaces that strengthen your brand and last for years.

Our Values

Honesty, quality and teamwork guide everything we do, from the first design sketch to the final handover.

Services

Design & Build

We turn ideas into modern, practical interiors, handling the design and the build together so everything fits perfectly.

General Contracting

We execute architectural plans with care, on time and to a high standard of quality.

MEP Services

Our engineers handle mechanical, electrical and plumbing work so your space runs smoothly and safely.

Multimedia & Technology

We build smart, well-equipped workspaces with the audio, video and technology today’s teams need.
